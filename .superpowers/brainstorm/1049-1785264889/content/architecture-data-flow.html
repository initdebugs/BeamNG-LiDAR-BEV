Design section 1 · Architecture and data flow

One perception pipeline feeds two views. Switching views never restarts sensors, changes planning, or alters the driving loop.

Live inputs
30 Hz

Semantic LiDAR array

3D returns, height, annotation class and sensor provenance remain intact instead of becoming two flat point buckets.

10–15 Hz

BeamNG actor states

Batched position, orientation, velocity and vehicle type. Registry refreshes slowly; poses update together in one request.

25 Hz

Planner + vehicle state

Chosen composite path, alternatives, AEB corridor, speed, controls and navigation intent.

Worker-thread scene assembly

Temporal surface builder

Short world-space history becomes a stable ego-relative road mesh, curb/boundary lines and sparse uncertain geometry. Old cells fade rather than jump.

Corroborated actor tracker

Ground-truth pose chooses the model, but LiDAR evidence controls visibility. A temporarily missed actor coasts briefly, then fades—never silently persists.

Scene-frame builder

Produces immutable NumPy/primitive data only. No QML or OpenGL object crosses the worker boundary.
