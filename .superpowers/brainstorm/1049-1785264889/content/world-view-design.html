01 · CRUISE

Low trailing camera

Ego sits in the lower third; look-ahead grows smoothly with speed.

02 · JUNCTION

Pull up and widen

More cross-traffic context appears as path curvature or branching rises.

03 · REVERSE

Rotate behind travel

Camera eases 180°; rear path and nearby boundaries become the focus.

04 · AEB

No camera drama

Keep orientation stable; threat, corridor and stop line change to red.

Always visible: ego, road/free space, chosen path, confirmed actors, speed and autonomy state.
Diagnostic only: raw points, candidate fan, sensor mounts, range rings, acquisition/poll counters and planner internals.
